Third-party component: FFmpeg

This portable package includes an FFmpeg Windows build so the application can run
without a separate FFmpeg installation. The bundled build identifies itself as
FFmpeg N-110463-g129c7bf53f-20230502 and reports GPL/version-3 configuration.

FFmpeg project and licensing information:
https://ffmpeg.org/
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

VideoBatchStudio starts FFmpeg as a separate command-line process. FFmpeg and its
libraries are third-party software and are not part of the VideoBatchStudio source.

Bundled fonts: Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC

The portable package includes Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC for Chinese text
watermarks. The font files are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The complete license text is included at fonts\OFL.txt.

Noto project and licensing information:
https://fonts.google.com/noto
https://openfontlicense.org/
